AIDaddy · one step ahead

May AIDaddy look at the pages you open?

Say yes once. From then on AIDaddy looks at every page you open in this browser and steps in when a page is about a deadline, a benefit or an official form.

What it does

  • It reads the page you are on. Not your other tabs, and not what you type into fields.
  • It decides on your computer first. Most pages are read here and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Only a page about life admin goes to the Engine: its address, title, field labels and the first 500 characters of its text. The answer comes back with the official sentence for every line.
  • It never reads a page with a password or payment field.
  • It fills a form only when you click, only in empty fields, and it never submits.

You can pause it on one site or everywhere from the AIDaddy icon in the toolbar.

What AIDaddy may fill in for you

Kept on this computer only. Leave a field empty and AIDaddy never fills it. It never fills an identity number, a bank account or a password.

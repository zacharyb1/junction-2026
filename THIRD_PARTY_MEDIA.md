These videos are edited stand-ins for the `/avatar` concept page, all made from that one clip: the idle clips re-render its rest frame with synthetic breathing, head movement and blinks taken from the same clip, and the speaking clips reuse its real gestures. In the prepared-answer videos marked `"lipSync": "musetalk"` in `public/avatar/desk/presence.json`, the mouth region is generated by the MuseTalk model (notice in `scripts/avatar/MUSETALK_NOTICE.txt`) to match the synthetic voice. The man shown is a stock performer. He is not a team member, did not say the synthetic words played over the footage, and does not endorse this project. Do not reuse the videos outside this project; take the original from Pexels under its terms instead.

The source file, its checksum and how each video was made are in `docs/AVATAR_DESK_ASSETS.md`.

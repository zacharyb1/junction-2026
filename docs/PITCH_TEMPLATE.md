# Pitch kit

Three artifacts, one story. Write the story first, then cut it three ways.

## The story (fill in)
- **Who** has the problem: ______ (specific person, not "users")
- **Problem** in one sentence, with a number: ______
- **Why now / why Europe** (ties to *European Tech Renaissance*): ______
- **Solution** in one sentence: ______
- **The wow**: the single moment in the demo where people get it: ______
- **Impact**: how many people / how much time or money / what changes: ______
- **Implementation**: what is real today (be honest, reviewers are engineers): ______
- **Next**: what you would build with 2 more weeks: ______

## 2‑minute video (hard max 2:00)
Record screen + voice. No intro animations. Cut hard.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Problem. One sentence, one number. Show the pain if you can. |
| 0:15–0:30 | "We built X, which lets Y do Z." Show the landing screen. |
| 0:30–1:25 | Live demo of the happy path. Narrate what the user wants, not what the UI does. End on the wow. |
| 1:25–1:45 | How it works (one diagram), what is real. |
| 1:45–2:00 | Impact + what's next + team names. |

## 3‑minute stage pitch (Top 5)
Same beats, slower, with a live demo on stage from your own laptop. Mic is cut at 3:00.
Rehearse with a timer. Plan the demo so that if Wi‑Fi dies you switch to the backup video without comment.

## Slide deck (PDF, ~8 slides)
1. Title + punchline
2. Problem (one number)
3. Who it is for
4. Solution (screenshot)
5. Demo (live, or screenshots)
6. How it works (architecture, one diagram)
7. Impact + business / "can it be a company?"
8. Team + what's next

## Judge Q&A prep
Prepare answers for: Why hasn't this been done? What is fake in the demo? How does this make money / sustain? What is the hardest technical part? Why your team?
